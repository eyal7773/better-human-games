Better Human

Games for the moments it’s easiest to lose it

Each game trains one real-life response. A few minutes on your phone, and the next time it really happens — your body already knows the way.

Boiling Point

This game annoys you on purpose. You practise noticing the heat as it starts, pausing, breathing — and choosing a response you won’t regret.
